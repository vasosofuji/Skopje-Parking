Delete your account

In the app

Open Settings, then Privacy & data, then Delete my contributor data, and confirm. Deletion happens right away.

On this page

Sign in with the username and password you use in Skopje Parking. Guest accounts have no password or personal details, so delete those from the app on the phone that created them.

What gets deleted

Your username, password, sign-in sessions, points, sign details, zone-label edits, price and availability reports and confirmations. Parking places and boundaries you published stay on the shared map without your name. Your licence plate and any AI key were only ever stored on your phone.

If you can't sign in, email contact@vasojevich.com from the address you want us to reply to, with your username, and we'll delete the account by hand.

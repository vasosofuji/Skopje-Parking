Why it exists

Most of us have circled Centar looking for a spot.

The information is out there, but it's split between operator websites, signs on poles and whatever a friend told you last week. Skopje Parking puts it on one map.

Prices come from the operators' published tariffs and from drivers who park there. Free-space reports disappear after 15 minutes, so the map doesn't keep showing a car park that filled up an hour ago.

How it works

From the moment you know where you're going to the moment you walk away from the car.

01

Pick a destination

Search a street or a landmark, or drop a pin. Nearby parking comes up sorted by distance or by first-hour price.

02

Check before you drive

Tap a pin for the tariff, the paying hours, the time limit and the latest report from someone who was there.

03

Pay by SMS

Choose your zone and the app writes the message for Gradski Parking or POC. You confirm the sign matches and send it yourself.

Live reports

One tap tells the next driver

When your phone has been still at a car park for about ten seconds, the app asks whether there were free spaces. Your answer turns the pin green or red for the next 15 minutes.

  • The question only appears once you've stopped
  • Each car park shows its tariff, hours and time limit
  • Prices reported by drivers are dated, so you know how fresh they are

Sign reading

The sign, typed out for you

Point the camera at a parking sign. Text recognition runs on the phone and turns it into a digital card with the zone, the price, the hours and the free days. It reads Macedonian, Albanian and English signs.

You compare the card with the photo and fix anything it got wrong. Only the details you confirm are published, and the photo is deleted once you're done.

Also in the app

All of it works in Skopje today.

Payment rules that match the sign

Zone codes, SMS numbers and paying hours come from the operators' own rules. Sundays and public holidays show as free where they are.

A reminder before time runs out

In zones with a time limit, the app reminds you 15 minutes before it ends, with the stop message ready to send.

A map that works offline

The streets of Skopje come inside the app, so the map still opens in an underground garage with no signal.

Add what's missing

If a car park isn't on the map, add it with a sign photo or by hand, draw its outline and say how many spaces it has.

Points for helping

Reports and new places earn points. They unlock app colours and an accent on the pins you added, and you never spend them.

Directions when you're ready

Go hands the route to Google Maps or whichever navigation app you picked in settings.

What stays on your phone

Your licence plate and your GPS history stay on the phone, and sign photos are never uploaded to Skopje Parking. If you add your own Gemini or Groq key for hard-to-read signs, the photo goes straight to that provider and the key sits in the phone's secure storage.

The server keeps your username, the reports you send and the places you add. You can delete all of it from the app, or from this site with your username and password.

Questions

Is Skopje Parking an official app?

No. It's independent and isn't affiliated with the City of Skopje, JP Gradski Parking or JP Parkinzi na Opshtina Centar. Payment rules come from what those operators publish, and the sign next to your car always takes priority.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free. You still pay the operator for parking, and your mobile operator charges the usual price for the SMS.

Can the app pay for me?

It opens your messaging app with the right number and message already filled in. You press send, so nothing is paid without you seeing it. Wait for the operator's confirmation SMS before you walk away.

Which phones does it run on?

Android. The Google Play listing is on its way, and until then you can install the APK from this page.

Does it work outside Skopje?

Not yet. The map, the zones and the prices cover Skopje only.

Do I need an account?

You can start as a guest. Add a username and password if you want to keep your points and contributions after reinstalling or switching phones.
